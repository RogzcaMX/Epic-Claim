Features & In-Depth Walkthrough

Explore every visual and technical detail designed to make claiming free games fast, lightweight, and frictionless.

Feature 01

Clean & Hassle-Free Feed!

Game artwork, synopsis, and claim button. No hidden or useless menus slowing down your flow. Clean and straightforward.

Just tap «Claim Game», confirm «Add to Library», and you're done. Can't read the whole synopsis? Tap the poster to expand it fully and check when the promo expires.

Home Feed
Unlimited Accounts
Feature 02

All Your Accounts in One App!

No matter how many accounts you own. Add them all and jump between them as often as needed. The only limit is keeping track of how many you have!

Feature 03

Already in Library! No Need to Re-claim!

Thanks to the red status badge indicating you already claimed it, you'll know that title is safely in your library. No duplicate claims needed.

Remember: confirm your claim by tapping the checkmark button so your local log records it and triggers the badge properly.

Library Reminder Badge
Notifications
Feature 04

Instant Notifications!

Whenever a new free game goes live, get notified immediately. View what's currently up for grabs in that exact instant. You can even customize notification alerts and sounds!

Feature 05

Direct & Unobstructed Flow!

Tapping «Claim Game» takes you straight to the promotional game checkout. No searching manually, no waiting on Epic's heavy web storefront to scroll endlessly. Just tap «Add to Library» and you're set.

Assisted Claiming
History
Feature 06

History & Claim Logs!

Find any claimed title using live search, or inspect the exact timestamp showing when each game was claimed on every individual account.

Feature 07

Settings & Preferences!

Adjust the language, toggle the app's Dark Mode manually or sync with system preferences, set notification frequencies, clear web cache, or wipe all accounts at once.
