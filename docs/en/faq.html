Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers extracted from the application to resolve any questions or issues.

Why is it stuck constantly loading?

This usually happens only with brand new accounts. You must sign in via your web browser to your Epic Games account and accept the «End User License Agreement». Once accepted, the app will load normally.

Why don't games get claimed completely automatically without touching anything?

Epic Games utilizes anti-bot protection (hCaptcha) and zero-cost checkout gateways that strictly require human confirmation. Epic-Claim eliminates the pain of typing credentials and navigating the storefront: it drops you directly at the order confirmation step.

Can the creator view my login credentials or passwords?

Not at all. This application operates with zero external servers, analytics, or remote databases. Session cookies are stored solely on your device using hardware-backed encryption (AES-256). If you clear app data or uninstall, everything is wiped permanently.

Why do I need to log in again after several days?

For security reasons, Epic Games periodically expires session tokens and cookies. When this occurs, simply use the Re-login Account option from the account switcher to refresh credentials without losing your local claim history.

How many accounts can I connect simultaneously?

There is no hard limit within the app. You can link as many profiles as you require and switch between them seamlessly with a single tap on the top bar to claim consecutively.
