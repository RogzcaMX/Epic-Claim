Behind the Project

Built by Rogzca Developer with assistance from Gemini AI.

How and why was Epic-Claim born?

One day, while claiming the weekly free games, a simple thought crossed my mind: "Is there an easier way to avoid all the annoyance of constantly logging in and out of different accounts?".

It was simply created so that lazy users, like its creator, have an effortless and less tedious way to claim Epic Games freebies. Especially tailored for those of us who manage multiple accounts and dread manually switching sessions in a web browser.

"Does this make me a developer? Not at all. My job was shaping the application, designing features, guiding Gemini throughout the process, testing in real time, and fixing bugs along the way. But hey, I learned plenty in the process."

Personal Use & Ethical Commitment

  • Personal and free project: Non-profit use. Any commercial sale or reproduction of this app is strictly prohibited.
  • Not a hacking tool: The app does not bypass security protections nor does it circumvent Epic Games payments. It only provides direct navigation to official $0 promotional offers.
  • Guaranteed privacy: No remote servers, trackers, or analytics. Your session credentials never leave your personal device.

Compatibility & Requirements

The app was built with broad support for modern and legacy Android devices:

Android 5.0 (Lollipop) and up Compatible with the vast majority of Android smartphones and tablets on the market.
Zero Google Services Dependencies Runs smoothly on de-Googled devices and custom ROMs without requiring Google Play Services.
